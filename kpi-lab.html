    In plain words

    Inventory accuracy tells you how often the computer is right about what’s on the shelf. Count a set of bins, then check each count against the system. A bin only counts as a match when the two agree exactly.

    The formula

    Inventory accuracy = bins that matched ÷ bins counted × 100

    Why it matters

    If the computer says you have 10 and the shelf has 4, you’ll promise orders you can’t ship. Every other report is only as good as this number.
